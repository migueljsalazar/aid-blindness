How we grade

One letter, one honest answer: if you reached for this today, would it reach back?

Grades measure how reachable help is — not how worthy the organization is. A food pantry earns an S because you can walk in today and leave with groceries. Section 8 sits at D not because housing vouchers don't matter — they're life-changing — but because most waitlists are closed and the wait runs years. We grade the door, not the room behind it.
