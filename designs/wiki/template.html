    how we grade

    one letter, one question: if a real person in need shows up today, how likely are they to actually get the help?

    an honest note

    grades measure how reachable help is for a person in need — not how worthy the organization is.

    a neighborhood food bank can earn an S while Section 8 sits at D. that's not because rent vouchers don't matter — they're life-changing — but because the wait is measured in years and most waitlists are closed. we grade the door, not the room behind it.

    every entry cites its sources, and every claim check links to where we looked. when the facts change, the grade changes.
